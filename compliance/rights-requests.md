# Handling data-subject requests (PDPL rights)

Deadline: reply within **30 days** of the request. It can be extended once, by up to 30 more days, only if the request needs disproportionate effort; tell the person before the first 30 days end and say why.

## Self-service (most requests)
| Right | What the person does |
| --- | --- |
| Be informed | Reads the Privacy notice (`#privacy`) |
| Access and copy | My account → Download my data |
| Correction | Opens their post → Edit post |
| Destruction | Deletes a post, or My account → Delete my account |
| Withdraw consent | Deletes or closes a post; deletes the account to withdraw all consent |

Exports and deletions are logged automatically in `privacy_log`.

## Requests by email ([contact email])
1. Log the request (date, who, what) in a simple sheet.
2. Confirm identity: the request must come from the email address of the account (ask them to reply from it), or they must be signed in and use the self-service tools.
3. Do it, or show them the self-service button. For deletion of a specific post they cannot reach, delete it from Supabase → Table Editor → `cases`.
4. Reply in plain language, in their language, within 30 days. Record the date of the reply.
5. If you refuse (for example, identity can't be confirmed), explain why and that they may complain to SDAIA.
